Questions

What people ask first

Are you real? How do I know this isn't a scam?

Fair question. We live in Tuscany, the business is registered and licensed here, and you can read our Google reviews or speak to past guests before you pay. We always talk on a video call first, so you'll know our faces before you land.

What's included in a week?

Your villa, airport transfers, the activities in your plan and one of us on the ground all week. Flights aren't included. Everything is listed in writing before you pay.

How does payment work for a group?

Each person pays their own share directly, so nobody has to chase friends for money. A deposit holds your dates, and the rest is due before arrival.

Is this a spiritual retreat?

No. There's no programme you have to believe in. It's time to slow down, eat well and be with your people. Many guests say they go home lighter, but that's up to Tuscany, not us.

Can you help with something small?

Often, yes. A driver, a day out, a table for a birthday. Ask us and we'll tell you honestly if it's something we do well.
